Recording checklist and staff practice
 Verify UI labels and exact configured values against the application on recording day.
 Capture a successful action, its new status and the corresponding history entry in the same take when
possible.
 Use separate test examples for due-soon, due-today, overdue, completed-on-time and completed-late
states.
 Keep the Workflow Designer scope label visible. Do not synthesize email notifications, direct uploads,
signatures or parallel approvals.
 Check exports for selected scope and row count. Use test-only examples for restore, deactivation and
destructive controls.
 Use the dated stress report for all capacity statements; p95 is not average screen-load time.
 After recording, compare all scene IDs, timecodes, subtitles and shot-list rows. Replace planning slots with
actual narration and capture durations.
Guided practice
Have each enrolled role perform its assigned step on one sample document. Ask the staff member to identify
the current desk, due state, available action and required input. End by checking release recipient, status and
full movement history.

# DTS Three Minute Overview

Taglish alternate cut. Use the linked master scenes; maintain the qualifications stated below. Read at approximately 130-140 words per minute and retime after the actual voice recording.

## 00:00-00:20 S01 S06

Kapag may nagtanong kung nasaan na ang isang document, puwedeng buksan ang DTS para makita ang status, current desk, deadline at movement history. Isang record ang sinusundan mula pagtanggap hanggang release.

## 00:20-00:40 S07 S08

Sa Admin Staff nagsisimula ang registration. Pagkatapos, ifo-forward ito sa configured Focal, na mag-aassign ng transaction, document type at responsible staff. Ang applicable processing clock ay nagsisimula sa assignment.

## 00:40-01:00 S10 S11

Sa My work, makikita ng staff ang assigned records. Dito bubuksan ang instructions, ilalagay ang external draft o revised file links, at isusumite ang output sa reviewer. Kapag may comments, may documented compliance cycle.

## 01:00-01:20 S12 S14

Dadaan ang standard example sa supervisor, division at department review. Ang clearance ay hiwalay sa actual release. Sa log out, itinatala ang recipient, releasing user at oras, habang nananatiling searchable ang history.

## 01:20-01:40 S15 S17 S18

Para sa reporting, may designated regional processing ng Consolidated CMR and PPR at configured recurring Special Reports. Ang automatic generation ay gumagawa ng obligation; staff pa rin ang kailangang mag-submit at kumumpleto ng review.

## 01:40-02:00 S19 S20 S21

Makakatulong ang Personnel Schedule sa availability at conflict checks. Sa Analytics at Charts, puwedeng tingnan ang workload, deadlines at turnaround, saka mag-drill down sa matching records para ma-verify ang summary.

## 02:00-02:20 S23 S25 S27 S28

Sinusuportahan ito ng account permissions, division focals, reference lists, audit log at backup tools. Ang key actions ay chine-check ng server. Sa configuration changes, kailangang malinaw ang authority at epekto sa existing work.

## 02:20-02:40 S29 S31

May workflow drafting at read-only simulation; hindi pa ito generic live execution. Sa verified six-thousand-document stress run, walang HTTP request errors at about two point nine seconds ang p95 full-state latency sa thirty concurrent requests sa tested machine.

## 02:40-03:00 S32

Para sa staff, ang practical routine ay simple: gamitin ang sariling account, buksan ang My work, basahin ang deadline at instructions, at i-record nang tama ang bawat action. Simulan natin ang guided practice sa approved test environment.

The overview does not replace the full staff demonstration or role-specific practice.
